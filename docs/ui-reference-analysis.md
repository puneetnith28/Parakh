# Visual Reference Analysis & Design Language Specification

## 1. Reference 1: Mobile Landing Page Analysis
- **Visual Rhythm:** Full-width alternating background sections creating dynamic reading pace:
  1. Hero: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with massive, tight uppercase headline and 3D colorful crystalline artwork.
  2. Features: Pure White with mobile mockup card and currency list.
  3. Conversion Value: Light Lime Green (`#9FE870`) with high-contrast calculator card.
  4. Physical Proof: Pure White with lifestyle photo and card CTA.
  5. Pill Strip: Horizontal chip bar with green action circle.
  6. Business Trust: Light Lime Green (`#9FE870`) with dark business card visual.
  7. Testimonials: Pure White with pastel testimonial cards (`#D9F2F7`, `#FDE7D9`).
  8. Manifesto: Deep Forest Green (`#163300`) with lime green text and photo.
  9. Security & Fraud: Pure White with 3D colorful padlock and feature list.
  10. Coverage & Footer: Light Gray (`#F2F4F0`) with country pills.
- **Typography:** Heavy bold compact uppercase for major titles (e.g. `MONEY FOR HERE, THERE AND EVERYWHERE`, `TRUSTED BY BUSINESSES SMALL AND LARGE`), clean sentence-case for descriptions.
- **Action Elements:** Bright lime green pill buttons (`rounded-full`), dark forest green icon circles.

## 2. Reference 2: Desktop Dashboard & Cards View Analysis
- **Layout Architecture:** Fixed left sidebar navigation with full-height white main canvas.
- **Sidebar Elements:**
  - Brand mark at top left.
  - Wide bright lime pill primary action button: `Send money` (full width inside sidebar, `rounded-full`, bold dark text).
  - Clean vertical icon nav links: `Home`, `Cards` (active), `Manage`, `Earn`.
- **Main Canvas:**
  - Generous whitespace surrounding the content.
  - Left-aligned clean page title (`Cards`, ~32px bold).
  - Centered hero/empty state:
    - 3D colorful origami paper airplane with iridescent violet/cyan facets.
    - Large bold compact uppercase headline: `LET'S ORDER YOUR CARD`.
    - Subtitle explaining value proposition.
    - Centered rounded lime green pill button: `Get your card`.

## 3. Reference 3: Desktop Multi-Step Onboarding / Mode Selection Analysis
- **Header:**
  - Brand logo on left.
  - Horizontal progress bar across the top with current stage highlight and step labels (`Email` — `Account type` — `Country` — `2FA` — `Password`).
  - Close button (`✕`) on far right.
- **Navigation:**
  - `← Back` link on left below header.
- **Centered Hero:**
  - Headline: `What kind of account would you like to open today?` (bold sans-serif, ~32px).
  - Subtitle: `You can add another account later on, too.` (~15px neutral gray).
- **Selection Panel:**
  - Two large symmetrical selection cards (`#F2F4F0` background, `rounded-3xl` ~24px).
  - Card 1: 3D colorful crystalline house illustration + title + subtitle.
  - Card 2: 3D colorful crystalline briefcase illustration + title + subtitle.
  - Subtle hover border/ring (`#9FE870` or `#163300`) indicating interactive readiness.
- **Bottom Disclaimer:**
  - Centered subtle text linking to policies.
